Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 12, 2026 · Applies to KinetBend for iOS

✔ No network · No collection · No tracking
KinetBend runs entirely on your device. It connects to no servers, collects and uploads no personal data, and contains no analytics or advertising SDKs.

01What we collect

Nothing. KinetBend has no accounts, no servers, and the app itself makes no network requests — it never uploads your inputs, calculations, or usage behavior.

You can verify this at the system level: with networking disabled, every feature (all five bend calculators, unit switching, language switching) works exactly the same.

02Data stored on your device

The following data stays on your own device and never leaves it:

  • Preferences — selected conduit type, units (inches/millimeters), app language. Stored in the app's local storage.
  • Calculation inputs — the values you enter (heights, angles, obstacle sizes) exist only while you use the app.

Deleting the app removes all of the above.

03Permissions

KinetBend requests no permissions at all — no camera, no microphone, no contacts, no location, no photos.

04Third-party services

None. The app embeds no third-party SDKs: no analytics, no ads, no crash reporting, no social login.

05Children's privacy

The app is not directed at any age group, and since it collects no data at all, it processes no children's personal information.

06Changes to this policy

If a future version changes any data practices, we will update this page and its effective date. Until then, the "zero collection" promise on this page remains in full effect.
