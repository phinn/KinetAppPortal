KinetBend for iOS · v1.0 · A conduit bending calculator that works 100% offline

✔ All features available offline · No account required
KinetBend computes exact mark distances for the five essential conduit bends: 90° Stub-Up, Offset, 3-Point Saddle, 4-Point Saddle, and Kick — with 1/16" fractions, decimal inches, and millimeters.

01How to use

  • Pick a bend type at the top of the main screen.
  • Enter your values — rise/offset height, angle, obstacle width, kick height.
  • Read the marks — distance-to-mark for each bend point, with a diagram showing where every mark goes on the conduit.
  • Units & language — switch between fractional inches, decimal inches, and millimeters at any time.

02FAQ

What is "take-up" in the Stub-Up calculator?

Take-up is the amount your bender "eats" when forming a 90° — the distance between the arrow mark and the back of the bend. It depends on the bender's shoe radius. KinetBend pre-loads common values; if your bender's take-up differs, use the value stamped on the shoe.

Why does the shrink differ from my reference card?

Shrink is a function of the bend angle, not the conduit size (Benfield tables: 22.5° = 3/16", 30° = 1/4", 45° = 3/8"). KinetBend computes shrink from the angle with exact math, so it stays correct at any angle — including ones not printed on cards.

Which conduit types are supported?

EMT, RMC and IMC from 1/2" to 4", with standard minimum bend radii built in. The math applies to any bender; radii only affect take-up constants.

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. Everything runs on your device. The app never connects to the internet and collects no data.
