Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 15, 2026 · Applies to KinetAppPortal for macOS

✔ No network · No collection · No tracking
KinetAppPortal runs entirely on your device. It connects to no servers, collects and uploads no personal data, and contains no analytics or advertising SDKs.

01What we collect

Nothing. KinetAppPortal has no accounts, no servers, and the app itself makes no network requests — it never uploads your app list, search queries, or usage behavior.

You can verify this at the system level: with networking disabled, every feature (scanning, pinyin search, launching) works exactly the same.

02Data stored on your Mac

The following data stays on your Mac and never leaves your device:

  • Launch history — which apps you launched recently (names and times), shown in the "Recently Launched" section; clearable at any time with one click in the app.
  • Interface preferences — language, view mode (grid/list) and similar settings, stored in system preferences.
  • Icon cache — app icon cache, used only to speed up rendering.

Uninstalling the app removes all of the above.

03Permissions

  • Reading /Applications and other app folders — solely to list installed apps and their icons; this is the core function of a launcher.
  • Launching other apps — happens only when you click an app.

Beyond these two, the app requests no other permissions (no camera, no microphone, no contacts, no location).

04Third-party services

None. The app embeds no third-party SDKs: no analytics, no ads, no crash reporting, no social login.

05Children's privacy

The app is not directed at any age group, and since it collects no data at all, it processes no children's personal information.

06Changes to this policy

If a future version changes any data practices, we will update this page and its effective date. Until then, the "zero collection" promise on this page remains in full effect.
